# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, and technical ability.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** — Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being considerate** — Think about how your words and actions affect others
- **Being collaborative** — Work together toward shared goals
- **Being professional** — Keep discussions focused and on-topic
- **Giving credit** — Acknowledge the contributions of others
- **Assuming good intent** — Interpret others' actions charitably
- **Providing constructive feedback** — Help others improve their work

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuroatypicality, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, caste, nationality, religion, or political beliefs
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate misgendering or use of "dead" or rejected names
- Doxxing or publishing others' private information without consent
- Threats of violence or incitement of violence
- Deliberate intimidation or stalking
- Sustained disruption of discussions or events
- Spamming or self-promotion

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at **hello@assistant.bd** or via the GitHub issue system. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers are obligated to:
- Maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter
- Take appropriate action against violators
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub, discussions, issues, pull requests, email, Slack, etc.) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns:

1. **Contact maintainers**: Email hello@assistant.bd with details
2. **Use GitHub tools**: Report via issue or private message
3. **Be specific**: Include dates, times, and details of incidents
4. **Stay safe**: Prioritize your safety; you're not obligated to confront the person directly
